Rutinapp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Rutinapp is an offline workout tracker for the r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine. This policy describes how the app handles information on your device.

Data we collect

None on our servers. Rutinapp does not require an account and does not transmit your workout history or preferences to a backend operated by the developer.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your phone or tablet:

  • Workout logs (sets, reps, hold times, optional notes)
  • Routine configuration and progression choices
  • App preferences (theme, accent color, language, sound and vibration settings)

This data is kept in on-device storage (SharedPreferences). It is not synced to the cloud.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

External links

The app may open external websites in your browser, for example:

  • Exercise demonstration videos linked from the Recommended Routine wiki (including YouTube)
  • Optional support or donation pages

Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Analytics and advertising

Rutinapp does not include analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or advertising.

Permissions

  • Internet — used only to open external links when you choose to
  • Vibrate — optional feedback for rest timers and hold timers

Deleting your data

You can delete individual workouts inside the app, or use Settings → Delete all data to remove your workout history, routine settings, and preferences from this device. Uninstalling Rutinapp also removes all locally stored app data.

Children

Rutinapp is a general fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Health disclaimer

Rutinapp provides training guidance based on publicly available fitness information. It does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting or changing an exercise program.

Changes
